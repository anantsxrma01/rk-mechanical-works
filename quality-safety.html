Quality Assurance

Standard quality inspection practices.

Our multi-stage quality control plan ensures that materials, weld joints, and structural members meet project specifications.

Inspection Stage Quality Verification Focus Acceptance Criteria
Material Identification Verifying mill test certificates (MTRs), plate heat numbers, and pipe grade markings. Complete traceability to design codes (IS 2062, ASTM A106, BS 3059).
Drawing Verification Cross-checking shop fabrication drawings against latest approved client revisions. Zero dimensional discrepancy before material cutting begins.
Edge Prep & Beveling Inspecting bevel angles, root faces, and surface cleanliness prior to fit-up. Smooth mechanical/ground bevels free of slag, rust, oil, or laminations.
Fit-Up Inspection Checking root gaps, alignment, tack welds, and squareness using calibrated gauges. Adherence to approved Welding Procedure Specifications (WPS).
Welding Quality Control Monitoring electrode baking, pre-heat temperatures, current, and interpass temperatures. Execution exclusively by qualified welders under supervision.
Visual Weld Inspection 100% visual check for undercut, porosity, overlap, spatter, and excessive reinforcement. Smooth, uniform weld beads meeting structural and piping standards.
Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Dye Penetrant Testing (DPT) and Radiography (RT) coordination where specified by client. Third-party inspectorate clearance according to code requirements.
Hydrostatic Pressure Testing Pressurizing completed piping and pressure parts to specified test pressures. Zero pressure drop and zero leak observed on calibrated test gauges.
Dimensional Punch-List Final overall length, diagonal, hole pitch, and flange orientation inspection. Verification against approved fabrication tolerance limits.

Note: Specific quality-control activities and third-party inspections are implemented in accordance with client project specifications.

Health, Safety & Environment

Safety as an operational priority.

Industrial fabrication and site erection involve inherent heavy engineering hazards. We mandate comprehensive safety controls on every project site.

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

  • Mandatory standard PPE on site: safety helmets with chin straps, steel-toe safety boots, and high-visibility reflective vests.
  • Task-specific protection: leather welding aprons, welding face shields, grinding visors, cut-resistant gloves, and earplugs.
  • Double-lanyard safety harnesses with shock absorbers for all work above 1.8 meters.

2. Permit-to-Work & Job Safety (JSA)

  • Zero work commenced without an authorized, site-specific Permit-to-Work (PTW).
  • Conducting daily morning tool-box talks (TBT) to brief crews on immediate task hazards.
  • Job Safety Analysis (JSA) prepared for non-routine heavy lifts and critical tie-in activities.

3. Work-at-Height & Scaffolding

  • Erecting only certified, tagged scaffolding with double guardrails, toe boards, and safe ladder access.
  • 100% tie-off enforcement to independent lifeline anchor points during elevated assembly.
  • Demarcating drop zones and tool lanyards to prevent dropped-object hazards below.

4. Confined Space & Hot Work Controls

  • Mandatory atmospheric testing (O2, combustible gases, toxic vapors) prior to boiler drum or ESP entry.
  • Continuous forced-air mechanical ventilation and dedicated external standby rescue attendants.
  • Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) verification ensuring positive electrical and mechanical isolation.
  • Fire extinguishers, fire blankets, and dedicated fire-watch personnel during all hot work cutting/welding.
