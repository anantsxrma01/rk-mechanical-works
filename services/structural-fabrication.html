Structural Integrity

The framework holding industrial plants together.

Industrial structural steelwork forms the essential load-bearing framework that supports heavy process machinery, boilers, electrostatic precipitators, piping networks, and plant operating personnel. Without robust, accurately aligned structural systems, equipment cannot be installed or safely maintained.

RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. provides complete structural steel fabrication and erection services based on client structural engineering drawings. We transform structural steel sections into high-strength, dimensionally verified industrial frameworks.

Workmanship Flow

Complete 12-step structural fabrication process.

01. Drawing Review

Detailed verification of shop drawings, member schedules, and connection details.

02. Material Selection

Incoming inspection of certified IS 2062 steel plates, beams, channels, and angles.

03. Material Cutting

Mechanical sawing, oxy-fuel, or plasma cutting to exact drawing dimensions.

04. Edge Preparation

Beveling plate edges, grinding root faces, and removing slag for sound weld penetration.

05. Fit-Up & Tacking

Clamping components, verifying squareness, camber, and dimensions with calibrated tools.

06. Controlled Welding

Executing continuous structural fillet and butt welds under approved weld procedures.

07. Drilling & Machining

Drilling bolt holes, slotting base plates, and milling load-bearing end faces.

08. Dimensional Check

Checking diagonals, overall lengths, hole center-to-center spacing, and tolerances.

09. Surface Preparation

Manual wire brushing or abrasive blasting to remove rust, mill scale, and spatter.

10. Protective Coating

Applying zinc chromate/phosphate primer and high-grade industrial topcoats as specified.

11. Dispatch & Rigging

Tagging, bundled loading, and transport to project sites with proper transit protection.

12. Site Erection

Safe crane hoisting, alignment, plumbing, high-tensile bolting, and touch-up painting.

Technical Considerations

Load path, connection & corrosion.

Industrial structural elements must withstand static dead loads, dynamic operating machinery vibration, and wind forces. High-tensile bolt torquing, gusset weld throats, base plate grouting, and moisture-resistant surface coatings are critical engineering considerations built into our fabrication protocol.

Safety & Site Discipline

Rigging safety & work-at-height controls.

Field erection involves multi-ton overhead lifts and elevated steel assembly. RK Mechanical Works enforces 100% tie-off safety harnesses, certified rigging tackle, wind speed limits for crane operations, and barricaded drop zones below erection bays.
