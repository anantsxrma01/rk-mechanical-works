Service Overview

The backbone of process energy and power generation.

Industrial boilers play an indispensable role in thermal power stations, sugar mills, paper manufacturing, chemical processing, and other steam-intensive industrial operations. Operating under elevated pressures and temperatures, steam generators demand metallurgical rigor, dimensional precision, and uncompromising statutory adherence.

RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. undertakes boiler-related engineering coordination, structural fabrication, field erection, and maintenance activities strictly in accordance with approved engineering drawings and our valid Special Class IBR Approval.

What This Service Means

Controlled execution within certified boundaries.

Rather than making unverified manufacturing claims, our service is focused on what matters most on-site: meticulous fit-up, certified welding, thorough pressure-part condition assessment, and regulatory liaison with boiler inspectorates.

Comprehensive Scope

Detailed scope of work across four pillars.

A. Boiler Supply Coordination

  • Coordination of industrial boiler supply through authorized manufacturers and vetted engineering vendors.
  • Equipment and component sourcing according to client technical datasheets.
  • Technical specification review against design pressure, temperature, and fuel types.
  • Material coordination for pressure parts, headers, tubes, and auxiliary machinery.
  • Procurement documentation support and pre-dispatch technical review.

B. Boiler Fabrication

  • Boiler-related structural fabrication including buckstays, casing panels, and column supports.
  • Fabrication of approved boiler components according to certified workshop procedures.
  • Plate cutting, edge beveling, and weld joint preparation using calibrated tooling.
  • Precision fit-up and multi-stage dimensional verification prior to final welding.
  • Welding execution under qualified procedures (WPS) by certified high-pressure welders.
  • Fabrication documentation, weld traceability logs, and inspection reports.

C. Boiler Erection

  • Site inspection, benchmark verification, and foundation readiness assessment.
  • Rigging, heavy lifting, and equipment positioning using certified cranes.
  • Accurate alignment, plumb check, and leveling of structural columns and drum saddles.
  • Boiler pressure-part assembly: steam drum, water walls, headers, and downcomers.
  • Piping tie-in connections, drain and vent lines, and auxiliary equipment installation.
  • Statutory inspection coordination and hydrostatic pressure testing preparation.

D. Boiler Maintenance

  • Preventive annual maintenance and planned shutdown inspection packages.
  • Emergency breakdown troubleshooting, tube leak identification, and plug/weld repair.
  • Ultrasonic thickness gauging and pressure-part condition assessment.
  • Economizer, superheater, and bed coil bank inspection and tube segment replacement.
  • Boiler auxiliary servicing: soot blowers, air preheaters, fans, and damper mechanisms.
  • Mountings and fittings refurbishment: safety valves, water level gauges, and blowdown valves.

Erection Methodology

9-stage boiler erection sequencing.

01. Site & Foundation Inspection

Verifying civil benchmarks, foundation pocket dimensions, anchor bolts, and site crane access.

02. Structural Steel Erection

Erecting supporting steel columns, cross bracing, drum supporting beams, and access galleries.

03. Drum Lifting & Positioning

Rigging and precision hoisting of steam drum and mud drum onto support cradles and plumb alignment.

04. Water Wall & Header Assembly

Positioning lower and upper headers, hanging water wall panels, and tack fit-up of membrane walls.

05. Superheater & Economizer Fitment

Lifting coil bundles, positioning within flue gas passes, and connecting inlet/outlet headers.

06. High-Pressure Butt Welding

Executing qualified GTAW/SMAW weld procedures with pre-heating and weld traceability logs.

07. Piping & Auxiliary Tie-Ins

Connecting main steam lines, feed water lines, soot blower piping, safety valves, and drain manifolds.

08. Hydrostatic Testing

Conducting hydrostatic pressure testing under applicable IBR rules in coordination with the inspectorate.

09. Commissioning Readiness

Assisting with refractory curing, alkali boil-out, steam blowing support, and final handover.

Engineering Considerations

Thermal expansion & statutory rigor.

Boiler components undergo severe thermal cycling. Design clearances, constant load spring hangers, expansion guides, and drum expansion indicators must move freely without binding. Our erection teams verify expansion allowances against manufacturer thermal movement calculations before hydro-test.

Quality & Safety Protocols

Zero tolerance for shortcuts.

All pressure boundary work adheres strictly to applicable Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR 1950) requirements. Welder qualifications, electrode baking, purge shielding, radiography/dye-penetrant examination (where specified), and continuous confined space ventilation are enforced.
