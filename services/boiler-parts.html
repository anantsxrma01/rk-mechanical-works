Pressure Integrity

High-pressure metallurgy where quality dictates uptime.

Boiler pressure parts operate under intense radiant heat, extreme internal steam pressure, erosive fly ash velocity, and aggressive chemical environments. Tube leaks are the leading cause of unscheduled power plant and process boiler tripping.

RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. coordinates the supply, custom workshop fabrication, tube bending, and site replacement of certified boiler pressure parts. Every component adheres strictly to specified ASME / IBR material grades, correct wall thickness allowances, and full material test report (MTR) traceability.

Quality Assurance

We ensure seamless cold and hot bending without excessive ovality or wall thinning. Tube banks are pressure tested and checked for dimensional pitch before dispatch and site fitment.

Three Critical Coil Verticals

Economizer, bed and superheater assemblies.

Feedwater Preheating

Economizer Coils

Recovering residual thermal energy from outgoing flue gas to preheat incoming feedwater before it reaches the steam drum. Every 6°C rise in feedwater temperature delivers approximately 1% boiler thermal efficiency gain.

  • Continuous hairpin loop and bare/finned tube bank configurations.
  • In-line or staggered tube pitch arrangements to optimize gas velocity.
  • Supply coordination of carbon steel seamless tubes (BS 3059 / ASTM A210).
  • Erosion shield fitment on leading tube bends exposed to ash impingement.
  • Site extraction of failed banks and installation of new replacement modules.
Fluidized Bed Heat Transfer

Bed Coils (AFBC / CFBC)

Immersed directly within the high-temperature turbulent fluidized sand/ash bed of atmospheric and circulating fluidized bed boilers. Controls bed temperature while absorbing intense radiant and convective heat.

  • Heavy-wall seamless alloy or carbon steel tubes resisting severe bed erosion.
  • Supply and welding of stainless steel protective erosion studs or half-sleeves.
  • Precision bend radius maintenance to prevent turbulent localized tube thinning.
  • Replacement of worn bed coil loops during scheduled inter-campaign shutdowns.
  • Header stub cut-out, alignment, weld fit-up, and hydro-testing.
High-Temperature Steam

Superheater Coils

Converting wet saturated steam into superheated dry steam at elevated temperatures (typically 400°C to 540°C) to maximize steam turbine enthalpy and prevent turbine blade water-droplet erosion.

  • Primary (convective) and secondary (radiant/pendant) superheater banks.
  • High-temperature creep-resistant alloy steels (ASTM A213 T11, T22, T91).
  • Certified GTAW/TIG root pass welding with internal argon purging.
  • Alignment of hanging support clips, spacer lugs, and header terminal stubs.
  • Replacement of sagged, overheated, or creep-ruptured superheater loops.
Technical Comparison

Pressure-part comparison at a glance.

Pressure Component Primary Thermal Function Typical Industrial Applications Material Specification Focus
Economizer Coils Feedwater preheating via flue-gas waste heat recovery. Industrial boilers, power plant boilers, waste heat boilers (WHRB). Carbon steel seamless tubing (ASTM A210 Gr A1 / BS 3059).
Bed Coils Direct heat absorption & bed temperature moderation. AFBC & CFBC fluidized bed boilers (sugar, power, paper). Heavy-wall carbon or alloy steel with anti-wear studs/shields.
Superheater Coils Steam temperature elevation above saturation point. Power co-generation boilers, process steam superheating. Low-alloy & chrome-moly creep-resistant steels (T11 / T22 / T91).
Boiler Headers & Tubes Fluid distribution, steam generation, downcomer circulation. Water wall circuits, generating banks, riser systems. Seamless high-pressure carbon steel piping & forged nozzles.
