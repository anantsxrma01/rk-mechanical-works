Turnaround Engineering

Maximizing plant reliability through planned maintenance.

An industrial plant shutdown (or turnaround) is a planned period during which production is halted to allow intensive internal inspection, mechanical overhaul, component replacement, and statutory inspections that cannot take place during active plant operation.

In power utilities, sugar mills, paper factories, and chemical processing facilities, disciplined execution during the shutdown window is essential to avoid premature restarts, unplanned tripping, and costly secondary downtime. RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. provides complete mechanical manpower and technical coordination to execute critical overhaul packages safely.

Honest Service Commitment

Realistic scheduling & site safety.

Rather than promising unrealistic zero-downtime guarantees, we focus on what genuinely delivers success: thorough pre-shutdown resource mobilization, clear daily task sequencing, strict Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) protocols, and multi-shift mechanical supervision.

Maintenance Scope

Detailed scope of turnaround services.

A. Mechanical Maintenance

  • Complete mechanical troubleshooting, vibration mitigation, and equipment condition checks.
  • Dismantling, inspection, re-bushing, seal replacement, and alignment of heavy machinery.
  • Industrial valve overhauls: gate, globe, check, and control valves (gland packing, lapping, testing).
  • Pump and motor coupling realignment using precision dial gauges and laser alignment tools.
  • Structural repair, corroded member replacement, and walkway/platform reinforcement.

B. Boiler & Auxiliary Overhauls

  • Thorough boiler pressure-part internal and external visual inspection.
  • Ultrasonic tube thickness survey to detect thinning, erosion, and overheating hotspots.
  • Economizer, superheater, and bed coil bank extraction, segment replacement, and hydro-testing.
  • Air preheater (APH) basket/tube cleaning, gas duct patch-welding, and expansion joint replacement.
  • Soot blower element servicing, nozzle alignment, and mechanical drive maintenance.
  • Boiler mountings and safety valve overhaul prior to statutory inspection.

C. Plant Shutdown Support

  • Assisting plant engineering managers with work-scope identification and bill of quantities (BOQ).
  • Comprehensive manpower planning: certified high-pressure welders, fabricators, riggers, fitters.
  • Pre-shutdown mobilization of specialized tools, portable welding sets, chain blocks, and rigging tackle.
  • Structured shift reporting, daily progress tracking against milestone schedules, and bottleneck clearance.
  • Post-turnaround technical punch-list completion, cold testing support, and handover documentation.

D. Safety & Isolation Discipline

  • Strict enforcement of plant Permit-to-Work (PTW) procedures and Job Safety Analysis (JSA).
  • Comprehensive Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) verification before any mechanical line breaking.
  • Confined space atmospheric testing, forced air ventilation, and continuous standby personnel.
  • Fire-watch teams and spark containment blankets during elevated hot work cutting and welding.
Execution Methodology

Structured 9-step shutdown execution framework.

01. Pre-Shutdown Planning

Confirming task lists, material availability, crane access, safety permits, and crew sizes.

02. Site Mobilization

Deploying tools, scaffolding, welding machines, gas banks, and skilled mechanics to site.

03. Isolation & Safety Prep

Lockout/tagout, blind insertion, line purging, confined space ventilation, and safety briefings.

04. Inspection & Dismantling

Carefully opening equipment casings, unbolting flanges, extracting tube bundles, and logging defects.

05. Repair & Fabrication

Cutting out worn sections, fabricating replacement spools/plates, and weld joint preparation.

06. Component Replacement

Installing new coils, pipes, valves, plates, bearings, gaskets, and torqueing connections to spec.

07. Reassembly & Alignment

Closing equipment casings, re-installing insulation, precision alignment of rotating machinery.

08. Inspection & Testing

Executing hydrostatic pressure tests, cold mechanical rotation, and leak checks.

09. Handover & Restart Support

Removing isolations, punch-list sign-off, and providing standby technical support during restart.
