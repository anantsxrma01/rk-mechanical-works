Critical Fluid Conduits

High-pressure integrity where failure is not an option.

Industrial piping networks represent the circulatory system of modern manufacturing and power plants. Safely transporting high-pressure superheated steam, condensate, pressurized feedwater, process chemicals, compressed air, and cooling water requires uncompromised weld soundess, correct support engineering, and thermal expansion accommodation.

RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. provides complete piping services ranging from isometric study and workshop spool prefabrication to elevated field erection and hydrostatic testing.

Statutory Clarification

Accurate IBR applicability.

We clearly distinguish statutory requirements: high-pressure steam distribution and boiler external piping falling under the Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR 1950) are fabricated and erected strictly under approved IBR procedures and inspectorate verification. Non-IBR utility pipelines (air, cooling water, low-pressure condensate) follow relevant industrial process codes.

Comprehensive Piping Capabilities

Detailed scope across steam, process and utility lines.

A. Steam Piping Systems

  • Boiler main steam outlet piping connecting steam drums or superheaters to turbine manifolds.
  • High-pressure (HP) and low-pressure (LP) process steam distribution headers and manifolds.
  • Steam branch connections, pressure reducing and desuperheating station (PRDS) piping.
  • Steam isolation arrangements, bypass valves, safety valve exhaust piping.
  • Condensate drainage drip legs, thermodynamic steam trap loops, and atmospheric vents.

B. Process & Utility Piping

  • Industrial process-fluid piping for chemical reactors, distillation columns, and sugar evaporators.
  • Equipment interconnecting spools, pump suction/discharge lines, and heat exchanger nozzles.
  • High-pressure boiler feedwater piping from feed pumps through economizers.
  • Cooling water supply and return lines, cooling tower manifolds, and condenser headers.
  • Plant compressed air distribution networks, instrument air lines, and service water headers.

C. Component Supply Coordination

  • Seamless and welded carbon steel pipes (ASTM A106 Gr B, IS 1239 / 3589).
  • Alloy steel and stainless steel piping spools where specified in project drawings.
  • Forged pipe fittings: 90°/45° elbows, concentric/eccentric reducers, equal/reducing tees.
  • Weld neck (WNRF), slip-on (SORF), blind, and socket-weld flanges (Class 150 to 1500#).
  • Gate, globe, check, ball, and butterfly isolation valves with test certificates.

D. Fabrication, Erection & Testing

  • Pipe cutting, beveling, mechanical prep, and internal cleaning of spools.
  • Precision fit-up using pipe clamps, alignment pins, and root gap gauges.
  • GTAW (TIG) root pass welding followed by SMAW (Arc) fill passes by qualified welders.
  • Erection on pipe racks, overhead bents, hangers, clamps, and shoe supports.
  • Hydrostatic pressure testing using calibrated pressure gauges and hydrostatic pumps.
Engineering Terminology

Key piping engineering parameters.

Our piping fabrication and installation adhere strictly to core engineering fundamentals.

Piping Parameter Technical Meaning Practical Application in Execution
Nominal Pipe Size (NPS) Standardized designation for pipe diameter (e.g. NPS 2, 4, 12, 24). Determines line capacity, flange sizing, and welding bevel geometry.
Pipe Schedule (SCH) Designates pipe wall thickness (e.g. Sch 40, Sch 80, Sch 160, Sch XXS). Selected based on internal design pressure and temperature calculations.
Design Pressure & Temp Maximum operating envelope the pipe must safely contain. Governs hydro-test pressure (typically 1.5x design pressure) and flange class.
Thermal Expansion & Loops Length growth caused by high steam temperatures during operation. Accommodated via engineered expansion loops, spring hangers, and guides.
Welding Procedures (WPS) Formal qualification specifying electrode, preheat, current, and interpass temp. Mandatory for code-compliant welding and IBR pressure piping acceptance.
Hydrostatic Testing Pressurizing pipe with water to confirm joint soundness and zero leak. Conducted prior to insulation application in presence of inspectors/client QA.
