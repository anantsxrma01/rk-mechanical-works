Industry Applications

Sectors benefiting from our engineering support.

These represent our target application sectors based on our technical capabilities and equipment specialization.

Thermal Power Plants

Coal and biomass-fired thermal power stations demand rigorous pressure-part maintenance, steam header overhauls, high-pressure piping tie-ins, electrostatic precipitator (ESP) casing repairs, and planned annual unit overhauls to preserve heat rate and power output.

Sugar Mills & Co-Gen Plants

Bagasse-fired boilers operate during intense seasonal crushing campaigns. Our mechanical teams support pre-season boiler overhauls, high-pressure steam distribution headers, bagasse feeding ductwork, juice heater piping, and inter-campaign maintenance.

Paper & Pulp Industries

Continuous process paper mills utilize high-pressure chemical recovery boilers and auxiliary steam generators. Scope includes digester piping, causticizing line fabrication, stainless steel stock piping, and machine hall platform structures.

Chemical & Process Plants

Processing industries handle aggressive chemicals, thermal fluids, and utility steam. We provide certified process piping fabrication, equipment support bents, reactor access walkways, valve servicing, and leak repairs during planned plant turnarounds.

Food & Beverage Processing

Industrial steam generation for sterilization, evaporation, and cooking. Scope covers package boiler steam distribution headers, sanitary and utility water lines, air piping, and food-grade access staircases and platform grating.

Manufacturing Facilities

Automotive, heavy engineering, and general manufacturing plants require heavy machinery support structures, overhead crane runways, compressed air piping loops, cooling tower manifolds, and factory maintenance access walkways.

Steel & Metal Processing

Sponge iron DRI kilns, rolling mills, and smelters require waste heat recovery boiler (WHRB) casing fabrication, cooling water headers, flue gas ducting, ESP overhauls, and heavy structural steel platforms.

Industrial Utilities & Captive Power

Institutional facilities, industrial estates, and captive power plants rely on centralized steam and chilled water networks. Scope encompasses district steam lines, expansion loop fitment, condensate recovery manifolds, and maintenance support.
