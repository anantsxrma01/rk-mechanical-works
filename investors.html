Sustainable Industrial Engineering

Aligning with Thermax's philosophy of "Conserving Resources, Preserving the Future", RK Mechanical Works integrates sustainable practices across manufacturing operations:

  • Clean Solar Power: 1.2 MW rooftop solar PV installation providing 45% of workshop electricity
  • Zero Liquid Discharge: 100% recycling of hydrostatic test water through automated filtration
  • Decarbonizing Heavy Industry: Supplying biomass boilers and WHRB systems that have helped clients prevent over 350,000 MT of CO₂ emissions annually
