Precision Piping

IBR High-Pressure Piping & Modular Skids

Our clean-bay pipe fabrication facility manufactures shop-tested piping spools, pig launcher/receivers, fuel gas pressure reduction skids, and turbine interconnecting piping designed under ASME B31.1 and B31.3.

  • High Chrome Alloy Metallurgy: Specialists in P11, P22, P91, and P92 creeping-resistant steam lines
  • Prefabricated Modular Skids: Factory-assembled with valves, flow meters, heat tracing, and explosion-proof electricals
  • Hydrostatic Testing: High-pressure pressure testing up to 700 bar with electronic data logging

Environmental Technologies

Air Pollution Control Systems (APC) & ESP

Engineered to meet stringent Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) emissions standards (particulate matter < 30 mg/Nm³), our environmental systems protect both client investments and community air quality.

  • Electrostatic Precipitators (ESP): Multi-field electrostatic collection with high voltage transformer-rectifiers
  • Pulse-Jet Bag Filters: High-ratio filtering using PTFE and Ryton needle felt media
  • Self-Supporting Chimneys: Aerodynamically designed steel stacks up to 120m height with strakes & internal acid lining
