Industrial Solutions Architecture

Comprehensive capabilities.

Explore our six core engineering divisions. Every service is delivered with strict adherence to project specifications, quality control, and industrial safety regulations.

Service 01

Industrial Boiler Works

Boiler supply coordination, structural and component fabrication, erection sequencing, tube inspection, preventive servicing, and pressure-part maintenance within approved regulatory scope.

Service 02

Structural Steel Fabrication

Fabrication and installation of equipment platforms, pipe support structures, cable-tray supports, elevated walkways, industrial staircases, structural beams, and floor grating.

Explore structural fabrication ›
Service 03

Industrial Piping Solutions

Steam lines, headers, process fluid networks, and utility piping (water, compressed air). Includes cutting, beveling, spool fabrication, erection, alignment, and hydrostatic testing.

Explore piping solutions ›
Service 04

ESP Works

Mechanical fabrication of ESP casings, hoppers, internal support frames, alignment of collecting and discharge electrode structures, air-leak inspection, and turnaround overhauls.

Explore ESP works ›
Service 05

Boiler Parts Supply

Supply coordination, dimensional verification, replacement, and installation support for critical Economizer Coils, Bed Coils, and Superheater Coils for power and process boilers.

Explore pressure parts ›
Service 06

Shutdown & Maintenance

Planned industrial plant shutdown support: mechanical inspection, dismantling, component repair, fabrication, reassembly, and restart testing with strict safety controls.
