Procurement Support

Frequently Asked Procurement Questions

Information for vendor registration committees, technical procurement officers, and engineering consultants.

What are your standard manufacturing lead times for ASME pressure vessels?

Lead times vary with metallurgy and thickness. Standard carbon steel vessels (SA 516 Gr 70) typically range from 10 to 14 weeks from approved fabrication drawings. Exotic alloy vessels (Duplex, Inconel clad) require 16 to 22 weeks depending on forged component and plate procurement cycles.

Can prospective clients arrange a technical shop audit prior to tender award?

Absolutely. We encourage technical inspection teams, third-party auditors (TUV, Lloyds, Bureau Veritas), and client procurement committees to review our manufacturing bays, testing laboratories, welding qualifications, and QA records in person.

Do you provide on-site erection, heavy rigging, and commissioning services?

Yes. Our EPC and Field Construction division manages complete turnkey erection, including heavy crane tandem lifting, civil foundation interface, site welding, hydro-testing, pre-commissioning, and statutory inspectorate approvals.
