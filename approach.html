The 9-Stage Framework

General project execution methodology.

This outlines our standard working approach. Specific execution stages are tailored based on individual contract scope, client engineering drawings, and site conditions.

01. Requirement Understanding

Thorough technical review of client inquiries, equipment schedules, design pressures, temperatures, and contractual work boundaries.

02. Site Assessment

Conducting site visits to inspect physical foundation readiness, crane access roads, overhead obstructions, and tie-in coordinates.

03. Technical Scope Finalization

Aligning with client engineers on bill of quantities (BOQ), certified drawings, division of responsibilities, and project timelines.

04. Material & Resource Planning

Sourcing verified structural sections, piping, electrodes, and scheduling skilled high-pressure welders, fitters, and rigging crews.

05. Controlled Fabrication

Precision cutting, edge beveling, fit-up verification, and welding under qualified procedures (WPS) with dimensional checks.

06. Mobilization & Logistics

Safe loading, transit protection, and dispatch of fabricated spools, platforms, or equipment to the project site.

07. Precision Site Erection

Safe crane hoisting, accurate plumb and leveling, field joint fit-up, torqueing high-tensile bolts, and site welding.

08. Inspection & Quality Checks

Visual weld examination, NDT where specified, dimensional punch-lists, and hydrostatic testing in coordination with inspectorates.

09. Handover & Ongoing Support

Submitting weld logs, material test records, final punch-list clearance, and providing operational standby support.
