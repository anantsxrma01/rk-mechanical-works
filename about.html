Our Profile & Mission

Built on Precision, Driven by Heavy Metallurgy

Established with a visionary roadmap to manufacture complex process equipment and heavy steam machinery in India, RK Mechanical Works Private Limited has evolved into a powerhouse of heavy engineering, rivaling global benchmarks set by industry giants like Thermax and ISGEC.

Spanning modern heavy fabrication bays, specialized clean rooms for non-ferrous and exotic alloys, fully automated welding manipulators, and in-house furnace heat treatment, we execute critical static and kinetic equipment for refineries, power plants, petrochemical units, fertilizer complexes, and core metallurgy sectors.

Our Core Purpose

"To engineer and deliver zero-defect heavy mechanical systems with relentless adherence to international safety, thermal efficiency, and metallurgy standards, creating enduring value for our clients and sustainable progress for heavy industry."

Principles & Pillars

The Four Cornerstones of RK Mechanical

Metallurgical Precision

We deploy strict welder qualification records (WQR), procedural specifications (WPS), and 100% volumetric NDT to guarantee defect-free weld joints even in high-stress thermal applications.

Zero-Harm QHSE Policy

Human life and environmental stewardship precede everything. We maintain a zero-lost-time injury record across our manufacturing facilities and field turnkey construction sites.

Statutory & Code Integrity

Certified to hold ASME 'U' and 'S' stamps, full Indian Boiler Regulations (IBR) approvals, and audited by premier third-party global inspectorates like Lloyds, TUV, and Bureau Veritas.

Manufacturing Footprint

State-of-the-Art Fabrication Infrastructure

Built to tackle the heaviest challenges with cutting-edge machinery, massive bay clearance, and specialized metallurgical workstations.

Heavy Bay & Crane Handling

Twin overhead EOT cranes capable of synchronized tandem lifts up to 100 Metric Tons, with 14-metre hook height clearance accommodating massive reactor vessels and tall distillation column sections.

  • 100 MT Tandem Lifting Capacity
  • 14m Hook Clear Height
  • Direct Rail & Multi-Axle Highway Link

CNC Rolling & Automated Welding

Equipped with hydraulic 4-roll CNC plate bending rolls handling up to 120mm plate thickness, paired with automated column-and-boom submerged arc welding (SAW) rotators.

  • 120mm Cold/Warm Plate Rolling
  • Twin Arc Submerged Arc Welding Stations
  • CNC Plasma & Oxy-Fuel Profile Cutting up to 250mm

In-House PWHT & Clean Room

Calibrated bogie-hearth heat treatment furnace for post-weld stress relieving (PWHT) and solution annealing up to 1200°C, alongside an isolated clean room for stainless and exotic alloys.

  • 18m x 6m x 6m Bogie Hearth PWHT Furnace
  • Titanium, Monel & Duplex Isolated Clean Room
  • Hydrostatic Proof Testing Bay up to 700 bar
Quality Assurance

World-Class Testing & Certifications

Quality is non-negotiable at RK Mechanical Works. Every weldment, forged nozzle, and rolled shell plate is mapped in our digital traceability ledger with mill test reports, heat numbers, and non-destructive examination logs.

  • Radiographic Examination (RT): Iridium-192 & Cobalt-60 exposure pits with automated film processor
  • Ultrasonic Testing (UT / PAUT): Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing for thick weld joints
  • Positive Material Identification (PMI): Handheld XRF & OES spectrometers for instant alloy verification
  • Helium Leak Testing & Ferrite Testing: Sub-surface leak detection for critical high-vacuum reactors
