About RK Mechanical Works

Industrial execution.
Built on reliability and safety.

An independent industrial engineering, fabrication, erection, and plant maintenance service provider headquartered in Gajraula (Amroha, UP) and serving industrial facilities nationwide.

Company Profile

Practical engineering for demanding industrial environments.

RK Mechanical Works Pvt. Ltd. specializes in practical mechanical engineering, structural fabrication, industrial piping networks, electrostatic precipitator servicing, boiler pressure-part supply coordination, and comprehensive plant shutdown overhauls.

Operating from Avantika Nagar, Mahajan Basti, Gajraula, District Amroha (Uttar Pradesh), our operations span PAN India. We work within clearly defined technical specifications and approved regulatory frameworks, prioritizing site safety, dimensional accuracy, and transparent project communication.

RK Mechanical Works holds Special Class IBR Approval. All boiler-related fabrication, erection, and maintenance activities are performed strictly within the company's verified and valid regulatory scope.
